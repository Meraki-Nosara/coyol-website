## New Business Opportunity - Astypalea Drinks Distribution

Marion's friend in Astypalea (Greek island) runs a 30-year-old drinks distribution business with these pain points:

### Current Problems
1. **Manual ordering** - Clients order via phone/Viber instead of app
   - No real-time inventory visibility for clients
   - Clients can over-order (request more than available)
   
2. **Manual invoicing** - Electronic receipts on GOV GR system done one-by-one
   - Time consuming, no batch processing
   
3. **No low-stock alerts** - No automatic notifications when inventory drops
   - Orders to suppliers are reactive, not proactive

### Potential Solution
Distribution management app:
- Client-facing order portal with live inventory
- Automatic stock level alerts
- Batch invoice generation for GOV GR
- Integration with Greek government e-invoicing (myDATA)

### Context
- Marion is in Greece (Kos, yacht trip)
- Astypalea is nearby Greek island
- Similar to Meraki problems (invoicing, inventory tracking)
