## Nosara Go — NEW APP! 🚐 (11am-12:15pm Israel time)

Built complete electric shuttle app for Nosara in 1 hour!

### Concept
- Electric mini-buses on Route 160 (Guiones ↔ Nosara Centro)
- Like Astybus in Astypalea, Greece
- Reduces traffic, helps locals commute
- Charging station at Esh Bakery

### Features Built
- **Registration:** Name, email, phone
- **Live Map:** 4 demo buses running on Route 160
- **Real Route:** Loaded exact KML from Google Earth
- **7 Stops:** Guiones Main → Guiones North → Pelada → Gas Station → Esh Bakery → Esperanza → Nosara Centro
- **Book Ride:** +1 guest option, generates QR ticket
- **QR Ticket:** Scan to board bus
- **Recharge Credits:** In colones (₡2,500 / ₡5,000 / ₡10,000 / ₡25,000)
- **Payment Methods:** SINPE Móvil, Tarjeta, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal (no cash!)
- **Map Bounds:** Locked to Nosara area

### Design
- Land Rover Heritage colors (Keswick Green, Limestone, Sand)
- Cormorant Garamond serif font
- Clean, premium feel
- Logo: Green bus outline with "NOSARA GO"
- Slogan: "Dejamos la moto en casa" 🏍️🚫

### Technical
- **Stack:** Astro + Leaflet + Vercel
- **URL:** https://nosara-go.vercel.app
- **Domain purchased:** nosarago.com (not connected yet)

### Route 160 KML
Marion drew the exact route in Google Earth — 58 waypoints from Guiones to Nosara Centro.

### Pricing Ideas
- Regular ride: ~₡1,000 (~$2)
- Rush hour pass for locals
- Monthly unlimited: ₡30,000 ($60)

### Rush Hours
- **6-8am:** Downtown → Beaches (workers)
- **5-7pm:** Beaches → Downtown (workers going home)
