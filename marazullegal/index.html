Questions Raised in Public Reporting

Recent public reporting has raised questions concerning water, construction, electricity, and environmental matters. Below are the corresponding official records and current documented status for each subject.

Water

What has been questioned

Reports have questioned whether Mar Azul has legal water rights and proper authorization for its well.

Documented status

MINAE issued a 10-year water concession (R-2133-2024) for well CJ-135 in September 2024. The original well GA-334 was donated to ASADA Santa Marta.

View water documentation →

Construction

What has been questioned

Reports have questioned whether homes in Mar Azul have proper construction permits.

Documented status

Construction permits are documented by lot through Municipalidad de Nicoya, with CFIA professional registration and INS insurance policies.

View permit documentation →

Environment

What has been questioned

Reports have referenced environmental concerns and an OIJ inspection.

Documented status

SETENA environmental approval 2514-2019 was issued. OIJ inspection (January 20, 2026) stated no environmental damage was observed. A complaint is pending with no final determination.

View environmental documentation →

Electricity

What has been questioned

Reports have questioned electrical infrastructure and ICE service.

Documented status

Electrical infrastructure was documented through CFIA process in 2014 (OC 662440). ICE coordination for dedicated public connection is in progress.
