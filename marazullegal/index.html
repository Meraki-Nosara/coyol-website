Water Rights Water concession and well documentation
+

Documentary Record

The development record includes MINAE concession R-2133-2024-AGUAS-MINAE, issued in September 2024 for well CJ-135.

The record also identifies the donation of the original well GA-334 to ASADA Santa Marta and AyA technical study GSD-UEN-GAR-2025-02417 concerning water service arrangements.

Referenced Documents

  • Well drilling permit DA-0080-2022
3
Electrical Service Electrical infrastructure and ICE coordination
+

Documentary Record

The development record identifies electrical infrastructure installed and documented through the applicable CFIA process in 2014, before the condominium designation was created.

Mar Azul is also coordinating with ICE concerning a proposed dedicated public electrical connection. This is an infrastructure upgrade separate from the existing internal electrical systems.

Referenced Documents
  • CFIA Seal OC 662440 (2014)
  • ICE correspondence
4
Environmental Matters SETENA approvals and related proceedings
+

Documentary Record

The development record includes SETENA environmental approval 2514-2019. SINAC documentation confirms that the property is not located within a protected area.

An OIJ inspection was conducted on January 20, 2026. The inspection report states that no environmental damage was observed.

Administrative / Judicial Status

A criminal proceeding identified as Expediente 25-000373-0414-PE was filed in June 2026. The matter remains pending. A response requesting dismissal has been submitted, referring to the findings and records described above.

Referenced Documents
  • SETENA approval 2514-2019
  • SINAC certificate
  • OIJ inspection report, January 20, 2026

For Prospective Buyers

Independent due diligence is expected for every real estate purchase.

What should I verify before purchasing?

Ask your Costa Rican attorney to review title, condominium registration, applicable construction permits, water rights and service arrangements, environmental documentation, electrical arrangements, and any current administrative matters relevant to the specific property.

Does this site provide legal advice?

No. This site provides documentary information. It does not replace independent legal, engineering, environmental, municipal, or other professional due diligence.

Can I request supporting documents?

Yes. Additional records may be requested where publication is restricted by privacy or confidentiality considerations. Documents containing personal information are redacted before release where appropriate.

Because prospective buyers may encounter those reports during their research. The purpose here is to provide the underlying chronology and documentary context so readers can evaluate the issues for themselves.
