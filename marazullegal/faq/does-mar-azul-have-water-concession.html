Does Mar Azul Nosara Have a Water Concession?

Yes. MINAE issued a 10-year water concession for well CJ-135 in September 2024.
Concession Number R-2133-2024-AGUAS-MINAE
Well Identifier CJ-135
Issuing Authority MINAE (Ministry of Environment and Energy)
Date Issued September 2024
Duration 10 years

Additionally, the original well GA-334 was donated to ASADA Santa Marta. An AyA technical study (GSD-UEN-GAR-2025-02417) has also been completed concerning water service arrangements.

Source: MINAE (Ministerio de Ambiente y Energía)
