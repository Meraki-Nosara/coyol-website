Does Mar Azul Have Construction Permits?

Yes. Construction permits are documented by lot through the Municipalidad de Nicoya.

Infrastructure Permit

Permit Number PC-714-2023
Issuing Authority Municipalidad de Nicoya

Individual Home Permits (Examples)

Lot 30 PC-328-2021
Lot 2 PC-572-2025 (approved January 16, 2026)

Each home in Mar Azul has supporting documentation including:

  • Municipal construction permit (Municipalidad de Nicoya)
  • CFIA professional registration
  • INS insurance policy

A distinction exists between building permits for individual homes and earthworks or infrastructure permits. These are separate regulatory processes.
