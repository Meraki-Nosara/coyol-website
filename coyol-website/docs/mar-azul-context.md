# About Mar Azul — From Us, Not the Headlines

If you've Googled Mar Azul, you may have seen a critical article. Here's the context they left out.

## The backstory

Mar Azul was always planned as a condominium. The families who moved in early knew this — they agreed to the structure before building. What came later was finalizing the budget: how the cost of running a proper gated community — security, roads, water, common areas — gets shared among owners.

For the first stretch, while those details were being worked out, the developer covered all common area costs. These families lived here without contributing. When it came time to formalize cost-sharing — decided by vote, like any condo — a few didn't like what they'd have to pay.

## The irony

The same owners who built before final permits and lived here while others footed the bill are now the loudest critics. They're a small minority, outvoted by the rest, and chose to go to the press rather than accept the outcome.

## What's actually true

- **Water** is secured — 10-year concession in place. We've also donated a well to the local ASADA and are completing infrastructure for direct supply (already approved by AyA).

- **Permits** for early homes are being regularized — standard process when finalizing construction that started before all paperwork was complete.

- **Electricity** has been here since 2014. Records are being updated to reflect the condo structure.

## Bottom line

Mar Azul has solid fundamentals, transparent governance, and a clear path forward. A few owners didn't like the vote results. That's the whole story.
