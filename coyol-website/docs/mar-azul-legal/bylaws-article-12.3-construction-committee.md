**Source:** BYLAWS ADMINISTRATION AND CONSTRUCTION MAR AZUL DEC 15.docx
**Saved:** July 17, 2026

## 12.3 Composition and Appointment of the Committee

**A.** The Construction Committee shall be composed of:

Five members **to be elected by the Condominium Administrator**. The members shall have a professional degree or other experience in design, urban planning, architecture, engineering or some other field related to the functions to be performed, and they shall be appointed until said appointment is revoked by the Administrator or by the Condominium Owners Board. The members of the Construction Committee may or may not be Condominium Owners.

**B.** If any member of the Committee resigns from his/her duties on the Committee, or another vacancy occurs (through events such as death or other termination of services), such vacancy **shall be filled by the Administrator**, who shall immediately appoint a replacement.

**C.** The Committee may hire, appoint and remunerate those engineers, architects or construction professionals it deems necessary to assist it in the performance of its functions and tasks. Said professional or professionals shall support the work of the Committee through on-site inspections, appraisals, recommendations and supervision with respect to any application or construction work to be carried out in the Condominium or the Units. In particular, said professional or professionals shall review the plans submitted by the Condominium Owners in order to identify all the considerations and recommendations to be taken into account by the Committee.

## Legal Significance (for Alfaro response)

**Key points:**

1. Construction Committee members are **appointed by the Administrator** — NOT elected by the assembly
2. Vacancies are **filled by the Administrator** directly
3. This is a **bylaw-granted administrative power**, not an assembly decision

**Therefore:**

The cautelar measure (which freezes "acuerdos tomados en la asamblea de... 25 de febrero de 2025") does **NOT affect**:
- The composition of the Construction Committee
- The Administrator's authority to appoint committee members
- The committee's authority to set and collect construction fees

The committee's existence and powers derive from the **registered bylaws**, not from any assembly resolution subject to the freeze.
