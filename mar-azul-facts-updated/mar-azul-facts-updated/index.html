Published reporting & documented responses

Public reporting is part of the record. This section provides context and identifies the underlying documents referenced in response.

Documented information

Mar Azul's records identify construction permits for the homes referenced in the development record, including homes constructed before and after condominium registration. A key distinction is between building permits for individual homes and earthworks or infrastructure permits; these are separate regulatory processes.

Lot 30 (Villa Cazulini) is identified with permit PC-328-2021. Lot 2 is identified with permit PC-572-2025, approved January 16, 2026.

Referenced records
  • Municipal payment receipt — Lot 30
  • CFIA registration #945136
  • INS policy #7729510
  • Permit PC-572-2025 for Lot 2

Documented information

Mar Azul's records identify concession R-2133-2024-AGUAS-MINAE from MINAE for well CJ-135, granted in September 2024 for a 10-year period.

The development record also identifies the donation of the original well GA-334 to ASADA Santa Marta and AyA technical study GSD-UEN-GAR-2025-02417, which recommended water service to Mar Azul through ASADA.

Referenced records

Documented information

The development record identifies electrical infrastructure installed and documented through the applicable ICE/CFIA process in 2014, before the condominium designation was created.

Mar Azul is also coordinating with ICE concerning a future public primary electrical line serving the development. This is an infrastructure upgrade and should not be conflated with the existence of the internal electrical systems.

Independent due diligence is encouraged for every real-estate purchase.

Ask your Costa Rican attorney to review title, condominium registration, applicable construction permits, water rights and service arrangements, environmental documentation, electrical arrangements, and any current administrative matters relevant to the specific property.

No. This site provides factual and documentary information. It does not replace independent legal, engineering, environmental, municipal or other professional due diligence.
