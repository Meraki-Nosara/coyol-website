La Encantada

Equestrian Center

Nosara, Costa Rica

Where European Tradition Meets Tropical Paradise

Nestled in the lush hills of Nosara, La Encantada Equestrian Center brings world-class European horsemanship to Costa Rica's stunning Pacific coast.

"A modern barn with a timeless soul"

Our contemporary black metal barn, accented with warm rustic wood, houses 22 beautifully appointed stalls. Under the expert guidance of our Austrian coach Sandra, riders of all levels discover the art of classical English riding in a truly magical setting.

Our Services

🏇

English Riding Lessons

Classical European training for beginners through advanced riders. Develop proper form, balance, and communication with your horse.

🎯

Jumping

Train in our covered arena with professional jumps. Build confidence and skill with expert Austrian coaching.

🏠

Horse Boarding

Premium boarding in our 22-stall facility. Expert care in a beautiful, secure environment surrounded by nature.
