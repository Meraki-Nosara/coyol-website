Nicoya Peninsula

One of Five Blue Zones

The Nicoya Peninsula is one of only five Blue Zones on Earth, regions where people live measurably longer, healthier lives. Here, the pace slows, the air is pure, and the ocean heals.

More than real estate, this is a continuation of a legacy. Quietly crafted, deeply human, and meant to be lived.
