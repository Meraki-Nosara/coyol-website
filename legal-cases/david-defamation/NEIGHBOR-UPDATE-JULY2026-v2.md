# Update for Mar Azul Homeowners
## July 2026

---

You may have received correspondence from Sfera Legal recently suggesting the administration is in violation of court orders. This isn't accurate, and I wanted to give you a clear summary of where things actually stand.

---

## The Medidas Cautelares — What They Were

In January 2026, a court issued a provisional injunction (*medida cautelar*) that suspended the agreements from the February 2025 assembly. The measure took effect on January 27th once the plaintiffs deposited the required $32,000 guarantee.

The administration complied immediately. On February 1st, all HOA fees were reverted to the pre-assembly amounts — for example, Finca Filial 1 went from $1,570.53 back down to $367.96.

---

## What Happened Next

In March 2026, we held a new General Assembly with 100% quorum. That assembly approved a new budget with 83.49% in favor. This vote was protocolized by notary and is completely independent of the suspended 2025 agreements.

The fees you've been charged since April 2026 are based on what the majority approved in March — not on the injunction-suspended assembly.

---

## The Current Situation

On July 15th, the court issued a warning as part of routine procedure. What Sfera didn't mention is that this came *before* we submitted our response. We filed our full documentation on July 20th demonstrating:

- Immediate compliance in February 2026
- A valid new assembly in March with overwhelming approval
- Current fees based entirely on the new, legally-independent agreements

The court has not found any violation. We've complied with every order, and our legal position is solid.

---

## What This Means for You

Nothing changes. The current fees reflect what 83% of owners approved, the administration is operating properly, and we're actively pursuing collections against those who haven't paid.

If you have questions, feel free to reach out directly.
