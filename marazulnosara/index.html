The Community

Mar Azul is a private residential condominium of 35 homesites, set across forested hillsides overlooking the Pacific Ocean.

Each home is sited for privacy and connection to the surrounding nature — howler monkeys in the canopy, ocean breezes through open living spaces, sunsets that mark the end of unhurried days.

Nosara, Costa Rica

Located in the Blue Zone of the Nicoya Peninsula, Nosara offers one of the healthiest, most vibrant communities in the Americas.

World-class surf breaks, pristine beaches, and a culture of wellness — all within reach of international airports and modern conveniences.
