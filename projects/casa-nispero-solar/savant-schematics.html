3. PHYSICAL LAYOUT — DISPOSICIÓN FÍSICA DE EQUIPOS Sheet 3 of 4
EQUIPMENT WALL LAYOUT — UTILITY ROOM

INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS

  • Location: Indoor utility room, dry, ventilated, protected from direct sunlight
  • Wall: Concrete or masonry wall capable of supporting 200+ kg total equipment weight
  • Clearance: Minimum 90cm (36") in front of all equipment for service access (NEC 110.26)
  • Ventilation: Battery enclosure requires ambient temp 15-30°C; provide ventilation if needed
  • Height: Mount main disconnect and meters at 1.5-1.8m from floor (ADA/accessibility)
  • Grounding: All equipment bonded to main ground bus; ground rod at building entrance
  • Conduit: EMT or PVC Schedule 40; size per wire count and ampacity
