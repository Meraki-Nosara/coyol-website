You know the story: gringo developer arrives, drills private wells, builds walls, locals get screwed.

This isn't that.

**Barrio Los Ángeles, 2016:** 32 hand-dug wells. Most with 4 centimeters of water. Septic tanks 5 meters from where families drink. Children sick. Government response after 40 years of asking: nothing.

**2017:** Developer buys land. Drills well. Donates everything to ASADA Santa Marta. No conditions. Gone.

**Now:** 65 families with clean water. AyA just approved 20 more years of capacity. ₡81 million more infrastructure coming — all to the ASADA.

One developer. One decision. 40-year crisis solved.

I have the geological study. The donation deed. The AyA approval. Every document with official stamps and reference numbers.

This is what development in Guanacaste can look like. Maybe it's worth telling that story too.
