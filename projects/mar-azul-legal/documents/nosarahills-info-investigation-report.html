1. SCOPE OF INVESTIGATION
This report documents the technical and contextual evidence linking the domain nosarahills.info to David Riabov and his company Scratch Agency / Scratch Marketing, based in Toronto, Canada. The investigation was initiated following the identification of defamatory content targeting Nosara Hills, Mar Azul Condominium, and related business interests.
2. DOMAIN REGISTRATION DATA
The domain nosarahills.info was registered on May 14, 2025 with an expiration date of May 14, 2026. The nameservers are ns41.domaincontrol.com and ns42.domaincontrol.com, both associated with GoDaddy as the domain registrar. The domain resolves to IP address 206.223.180.218, hosted by Beanfield Technologies with servers physically located in Toronto, Canada.
3. SHARED INFRASTRUCTURE ANALYSIS
A reverse IP lookup on 206.223.180.218 identified additional domains hosted on the same server: scratchagency.ca, scratch.marketing, and scratchmarketing.com. All three belong to Scratch Agency / Scratch Marketing, a Toronto-based marketing firm.
Finding: The defamation site nosarahills.info shares server infrastructure with three domains belonging to Scratch Agency / Scratch Marketing. Four domains, one server, one operator.
4. IDENTIFYING THE PRINCIPAL
The purchase agreement for Lot 20 at Mar Azul Condominium identifies the buyer entity as Rancho Verde Riabov SRL, with David Riabov and Erin Green listed as managers. The contact email provided in the contract is david@scratchmarketing.com. The seller was Desarrollo Rancho Mar Azul Ltda.
Scratch Agency, operating at scratchagency.ca, is a marketing firm based in Toronto. The domain scratchmarketing.com and the email domain @scratchmarketing.com are associated with the same operation.
Finding: David Riabov, manager of Rancho Verde Riabov SRL, uses the email david@scratchmarketing.com — directly linking him to the Scratch Marketing infrastructure that hosts nosarahills.info.
5. CONTENT ANALYSIS
The website nosarahills.info operates under the title "Nosara Hills — The Real Truth" and publishes content designed to damage the commercial reputation of Nosara Hills and related entities.
The site features articles originally published by La Voz de Guanacaste in 2017 covering environmental proceedings related to Nosara Hills. These articles have been republished without authorization and stripped of their original journalistic context to serve as attack content.
The site also references Coyol Real Estate, Los Coyoles, Finca Mar Azul, and Marion Peri by name.
Finding: The content is not original journalism. It is recycled material repurposed for a targeted defamation campaign against multiple related business interests.
6. FINANCIAL MOTIVE
Rancho Verde Riabov SRL, owner of Lot 20 at Mar Azul, has an outstanding debt of $10,426 USD in unpaid condominium maintenance fees. The company has not made any payments for over 12 months. The creation of the defamation website coincides with the escalation of collection efforts against the property.
