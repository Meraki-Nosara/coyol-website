Meters are installed at each lot. Billing occurs monthly. Invoices reflect consumption, rate calculation, and supporting detail. This information has been communicated previously and the methodology has not changed.

(f)–(i) The authority to bill owners for consumption of common services derives from:
- The Ley Reguladora de la Propiedad en Condominio
- The registered Reglamento de Condominio

No Assembly resolution is required for the Administration to bill owners for consumption of approved common services. Your suggestion otherwise misstates the law.

Your characterization is incorrect. Water consumption charges derive from the existing approved budget, not from a pending future approval. ## 🎯 LEGAL ANALYSIS — Common Attack Vectors

**All 4 owners (Nigel, Rachele, David, Matthew) are building the same case:**

### 1. "Developer Obligations" Argument
- **Nigel:** Well electrification = developer cost, not HOA
- **David:** Well commissioning should've been done at delivery

- **Matthew:** Questions if new charges are shifting developer costs

**Defense:** The well was constructed. Operational costs (electricity, maintenance) are by definition common expenses under Ley de Condominios. Capital vs operating cost distinction is clear in Costa Rican law.

---

### 2. "No Assembly Approval" Argument
- **All four** demand proof of Assembly resolution authorizing:
  - The switch from ASADA to well
  - The water billing methodology
  - Any fee reallocation

**Defense:** Routine operational decisions (which water source to use, billing methodology for consumption) fall under Administration's ordinary management authority. Only *extraordinary* decisions (major capital expenditure, changing the Reglamento) require Assembly approval. Using existing infrastructure is not a "material change."

---

### 3. "Suspended Resolutions" Argument
- **David & Matthew** specifically ask for confirmation that decisions are not based on resolutions "currently suspended or under challenge in pending proceedings"

**Defense:** This is the key legal angle. They're trying to tie the well operation to whatever litigation is pending. Document clearly that the well activation is independent of any disputed resolutions.

---

### 4. "Regulatory Compliance" Argument

**Defense:**
