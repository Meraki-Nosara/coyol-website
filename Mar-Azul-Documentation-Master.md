## ELECTRICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

### Current Status
Connected via shared ICE macro meter. Each home pays proportional usage based on internal meter readings, in accordance with Mar Azul bylaws and Ley 7933 (Ley Reguladora de la Propiedad en Condominio).

### Upcoming
Following a coordination meeting with ICE in Liberia, an agreement was reached to proceed with dedicated public electrical infrastructure. High-tension line along the main road is in first stages of design and permitting.
