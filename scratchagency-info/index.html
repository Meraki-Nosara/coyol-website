The Story

In 2021, a Toronto-based marketing executive purchased a lot in Mar Azul, a private residential community in Nosara, Costa Rica. He selected a home design from the developer's catalog and signed contracts acknowledging all existing conditions.

After his home was built, he began a systematic campaign to halt further development in the community — apparently wanting no neighbors.

This page documents the facts, supported by contracts, government findings, and public records.

Media Manipulation

False claims fed to regional and national media:

False Claim #1

"Was not aware the development was ongoing while building his house."

THE TRUTH: He purchased Lot 20 in a development called "Mar Azul" — a planned residential community. His own construction contract (Nov 2021) references the development's infrastructure, roads, and common areas. Construction was visibly active throughout.

False Claim #2

"Construction permits were issued with a water letter from a source that was not connected at the time."

THE TRUTH: Water letters were issued by ASADA Santa Marta Beach (Feb 2024) — a legally constituted public water authority. The development's previous owner donated a well that now serves approximately 250 families in Nosara. Well permit DA-0080-2022 documents the legal water source.

These false claims were published by Voice of Guanacaste and other media outlets without verification — damaging the development's reputation and misleading potential buyers.
