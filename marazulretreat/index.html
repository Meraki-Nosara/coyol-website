A Place to Slow Down

Mar Azul Retreat is held at El Rancho, the original farmhouse of the Mar Azul estate. Five bedrooms under a palm-thatched roof. A pottery cabin. Gardens producing what we cook. No agenda beyond presence, practice, and creation.

El Rancho

The Original Farmhouse

Built in the traditional Costa Rican style with a soaring palm-thatched roof, El Rancho sits on a hillside overlooking the Pacific. Five private bedrooms. Open-air living. Gardens surrounding.

All activities — yoga, cooking, pottery, gardening — happen within steps of where you sleep. The retreat is the place itself.
